Biography

James Raymond Tillman was born in Decatur, Georgia and enlisted at age 21. Known for his steady courage and care for his fellow soldiers, he served two tours in Vietnam and was decorated for valor during the Tet Offensive. After returning home, James worked as a high school history teacher in Atlanta for over 30 years, sharing his experiences to educate future generations. He is survived by his wife, Margaret, and three children.
